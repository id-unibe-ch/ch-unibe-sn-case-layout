My lists

Adds the lists (prefixed “SN: ”) under Lists → My lists → Created by me. Open a ServiceNow tab first. Grouping: by assignment group (combined list: by type). Existing lists are skipped; only their grouping and position are updated. “Remove SN: lists” deletes all your lists starting with “SN: ”.
